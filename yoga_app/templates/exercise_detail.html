Step-by-Step Execution

  1. Stand or sit in an upright posture with your feet shoulder-width apart and spine straight.
  2. Raise your arms up, bending at the elbows at approximately 90 degrees.
  3. Begin smooth, controlled circular rotations through your shoulder joints.
  4. Maintain stable hips and keep your elbows positioned close to your sides throughout the movement.
  5. Exhale as your arms reach peak elevation, then slowly return to the starting neutral position.

Common Form Mistakes Detected by AI

  • Flaring elbows outward away from the torso (flashing red alert during live session).
  • Leaning the spine forward or hunching shoulders unevenly.
  • Moving too quickly without controlled range of motion.
